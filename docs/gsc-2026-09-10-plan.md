## Changes in this iteration

- Preserve the pixel URL with 27 clicks and 1,808 impressions. Add practical
  beginner examples, cell aspect-ratio calculations, and accurate export limits.
- Defer /pixel-art-grid-maker: the existing renderer already serves that intent.
  Reconsider only with a distinct workflow such as editable cells and palette
  controls, supported by query/page data and search-result comparison.
- Focus homepage copy on choosing the correct output rather than listing synonyms.
- Lead the overlay page title with its primary keyword.
- Prioritize pixel, drawing, and photo-overlay links throughout related navigation.
- Recover known duplicate and literal route-placeholder URLs with permanent
  redirects. Preserve query strings. Unknown destinations remain unknown rather
  than redirecting to the homepage. Normalize known trailing slashes and English
  prefixes, and consolidate HTTP/www on production hosts in the same response.

## Acceptance and follow-up

Run scripts/check-canonical-paths.ts and the production build before release.
After deployment, check representative redirect responses, canonical HTML and
sitemap.xml. Existing locale URLs and hreflang relationships stay stable.

Compare equal 28-day periods after recrawling, filtering each landing page and
then viewing its queries. Track pixel clicks, impressions, CTR and position
together; CTR alone can fall as a page gains broader exposure. Check that malformed
URLs move to redirected status. Do not promise a ranking or AdSense approval.
